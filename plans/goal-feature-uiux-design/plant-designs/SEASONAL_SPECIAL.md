## 28. Mai Vàng (Yellow Apricot) - "The Tết Spirit" 🇻🇳

### Quick Stats
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Tier | Special - Holiday |
| Difficulty | Medium |
| Tolerance | 3 ngày |
| Growth Duration | 21 ngày để bloom |
| XP Multiplier | 2.0x during Tết |
| Availability | 2 weeks around Lunar New Year |

### Availability Window
```
WHEN AVAILABLE:
- 1 week before Lunar New Year
- Through 1 week after
- Total: ~14-16 days
- Returns every year

2026 EXAMPLE:
- Available: Jan 22 - Feb 8
- Peak: Jan 29 (New Year's Eve) - Feb 2
```

### Cultural Significance
- THE flower of Tết in Southern Vietnam
- Yellow = prosperity, luck, wealth
- Must bloom ON New Year for luck
- Family tradition to buy/grow Mai
- Deeply meaningful to Vietnamese users

### Visual Description
```
Appearance:
- Bare branches with yellow blossoms
- 5-petal yellow flowers
- Traditional pot (đất nung or sứ)
- Red ribbon tied to trunk
- Occasional golden coin decorations

Animation:
- Petals gently falling
- Subtle golden glow during Tết
- Firecracker sparkles (New Year's Eve)
```

### Unique Mechanics

**1. Bloom Timing Challenge**
- Goal: Have Mai bloom ON Lunar New Year
- Plant early = may bloom early (bad luck!)
- Plant late = may miss blooming
- PERFECT TIMING = blooms exactly on New Year
- "Perfect Bloom" achievement

**2. Lì Xì Collection**
- During Tết period, collect "Lì Xì" (lucky money)
- Red envelopes appear on branches
- Tap to collect
- Premium currency bonus

**3. Family Blessing**
- Can "dedicate" Mai to family member
- Write blessing/wish for them
- They receive notification
- Strengthens real relationships

**4. New Year Resolution**
- On New Year's Eve, prompted for resolution
- Resolution becomes new habit/plant
- Special "New Year Resolution" badge on that plant

### Care Requirements
| Action | Frequency | Required/Optional | XP Bonus |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|----------|
| Watering | Daily | Required | +15 |
| Pruning | Day 10 | Required once | +20 |
| Bloom timing | Watch carefully | Skill-based | +50 if perfect |
| Lì Xì collection | Daily during Tết | Optional | +coins |

### Rewards & Achievements
- **Bloom during Tết**: "Xuân Sang" badge +200 XP
- **Perfect timing**: "Hoa Nở Đúng Ngày" +500 XP
- **Special achievements**:
  - "Tết Master" - 3 years of perfect Mai
  - "Family Blessing" - Dedicate Mai to 5 people
  - "Lucky Garden" - Mai + Đào together

### Lore
> "Mai Vàng là linh hồn của Tết. Khi những cánh hoa vàng nở, mùa xuân đến, và với nó là hy vọng, may mắn, và khởi đầu mới. Một năm mới tốt lành bắt đầu với Mai nở đúng ngày."
>
> *"The Yellow Apricot is the soul of Tết. When its golden petals bloom, spring arrives, and with it comes hope, luck, and new beginnings. A prosperous year starts with Mai blooming on the right day."*

## 29. Đào (Peach Blossom) - "The Northern Spring" 🇻🇳

### Quick Stats
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Tier | Special - Holiday |
| Difficulty | Medium |
| Tolerance | 2 ngày |
| Growth Duration | 28 ngày để bloom |
| XP Multiplier | 2.0x during Tết |
| Availability | 2 weeks around Lunar New Year |

### Cultural Significance
- THE flower of Tết in Northern Vietnam
- Pink = romance, life, vitality
- Ward off evil spirits
- Used to decorate homes
- Symbol of Northern Vietnamese identity

### Visual Description
```
Appearance:
- Elegant branches with pink blossoms
- 5-petal soft pink flowers
- More delicate than Mai
- Often in tall vase
- Traditional Northern styling

Animation:
- Graceful petal fall
- Pink glow during bloom
- Occasional butterflies
```

### Unique Mechanics

**1. Regional Pride**
- Users can choose Mai OR Đào (or both!)
- Creates friendly North/South rivalry
- Regional leaderboards during Tết
- Celebrate Vietnamese diversity

**2. Protection Blessing**
- Ancient belief: Đào wards off evil
- Tap Đào to "bless" garden
- Protected plants get temporary immunity
- "Guardian Bloom" effect

**3. Poetry Mode**
- Đào inspires poetry prompts
- Write spring poems
- Best poems featured (opt-in)
- "Spring Poet" achievement

### Rewards & Achievements
- **Bloom during Tết**: "Xuân Bắc" badge +200 XP
- **Special achievements**:
  - "Bảo Vệ" - Use protection blessing 10 times
  - "Thi Sĩ Mùa Xuân" - Write 5 poems
  - "Hòa Hợp" - Mai + Đào together (unity)

### Lore
> "Đào mang màu hồng của sự sống, của tình yêu, của mùa xuân phương Bắc. Người xưa tin rằng Đào xua đuổi tà ma, bảo vệ gia đình trong năm mới. Mỗi cánh hoa là một lời chúc bình an."
>
> *"Peach Blossom carries the pink of life, of love, of Northern spring. The ancients believed it wards off evil, protecting the family in the new year. Each petal is a blessing of peace."*

## 30. Bí Ngô (Pumpkin) - "The Harvest Guardian" 🎃

### Quick Stats
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Tier | Special - Holiday |
| Difficulty | Easy |
| Tolerance | 4 ngày |
| Growth Duration | 35 ngày để harvest |
| XP Multiplier | 1.5x during October |
| Availability | October (Halloween season) |

### Availability Window
```
WHEN AVAILABLE:
- October 1-31
- Peak: October 25-31 (Halloween week)
- Returns every year
```

### Visual Description
```
Appearance:
- Growing pumpkin on vine
- Orange, plump, traditional shape
- Can be carved after mature!
- Optional: Jack-o'-lantern mode

Animation:
- Vine creeping growth
- Pumpkin swelling animation
- Flickering candle (if carved)
- Spooky fog during Halloween week
```

### Unique Mechanics

**1. Carving System**
- After mature, can "carve" pumpkin
- Choose from face designs:
  - Happy 😊
  - Spooky 😈
  - Silly 😜
  - Custom (draw your own)
- Carved pumpkin glows at night

**2. Trick or Treat**
- During Halloween week, random "visitors"
- Tiny ghosts, bats, witches appear
- Tap them for candy (currency)
- "Trick or Treat" mini-event

**3. Spooky Garden Mode**
- Garden gets Halloween decoration
- Fog, cobwebs, bats flying
- Other plants get costume accessories
- Fun, not scary

**4. Harvest Festival**
- Multiple pumpkins = "Pumpkin Patch" bonus
- Can gift carved pumpkins to friends
- "Best Pumpkin" community vote

### Care Requirements
| Action | Frequency | Required/Optional | XP Bonus |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|----------|
| Watering | Daily | Required | +12 |
| Vine training | Day 15 | Optional | +15 |
| Carving | After mature | Optional | +30 |
| Candy collection | Halloween week | Optional | +candy |

### Rewards & Achievements
- **First pumpkin**: "Harvest Time" badge +120 XP
- **Special achievements**:
  - "Pumpkin King" - Carve 10 pumpkins
  - "Pumpkin Patch" - 5+ pumpkins at once
  - "Master Carver" - All carving designs unlocked
  - "Candy Collector" - 100 candy during trick or treat

### Lore
> "Bí Ngô là người bảo vệ mùa thu hoạch. Khi được khắc và thắp sáng, nó đứng gác cửa giữa thế giới sống và thế giới linh hồn. Trong đêm Halloween, nó chào đón khách và xua đuổi bóng tối."
>
> *"The Pumpkin is the guardian of harvest. When carved and lit, it stands guard at the door between the world of the living and the world of spirits. On Halloween night, it welcomes guests and wards off darkness."*

## 32. Hoa Sen (Lotus) - "The Enlightened Bloom" 🪷

### Quick Stats
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Tier | Special - Spiritual |
| Difficulty | Medium-Hard |
| Tolerance | 1 ngày (water plant) |
| Growth Duration | 66 ngày để bloom |
| XP Multiplier | 1.8x |
| Availability | Always (but requires special unlock) |

### Unlock Condition
```
REQUIREMENT: Create "Pond" in garden
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Reach Level 10
- Earn 1000 coins OR complete "Water Path" quest
- Purchase/unlock Pond tile
- Only ONE pond per garden (precious!)
- Lotus can only grow in pond
```

### Visual Description
```
Appearance:
- Pond tile with water
- Lotus emerges from muddy bottom
- Large round leaves (lily pads)
- Pink/white lotus bloom
- Koi fish swim below
- Dragonflies visit

Animation:
- Water ripples
- Lotus opens at dawn
- Lotus closes at dusk
- Koi swimming patterns
- Occasional frog appearance
```

### Unique Mechanics

**1. Mud to Beauty Journey**
```
Visual Progression:
Day 1-14:  Murky, muddy water only
Day 15-28: Water clearing slightly
Day 29-40: First lily pad emerges
Day 41-50: More pads, stem rising
Day 51-60: Bud forming above water
Day 61-66: Bloom opening slowly
Day 66+:   Full lotus bloom

METAPHOR: Beauty emerges from mud
This IS the teaching
```

**2. Daily Open/Close Cycle**
- Lotus opens in morning (6 AM - 10 AM)
- Lotus closes in evening (6 PM - 10 PM)
- Encourages morning and evening check
- "Breathing flower" effect
- Creates daily rhythm

**3. Meditation Integration**
- Lotus provides "meditation space"
- "Sit with Lotus" feature
- Mini-meditation prompts
- Calm sounds (water, nature)
- XP for meditation time

**4. Koi Fish Companions**
- Pond has koi fish
- Fish grow with lotus care
- Can name your koi
- Fish bring small gifts
- "Koi Keeper" achievement

**5. Enlightenment XP**
- Lotus generates "Enlightenment XP"
- Different from regular XP
- Used for wisdom unlocks
- Philosophical content

### Care Requirements
| Action | Frequency | Required/Optional | XP Bonus |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|----------|
| Water quality | Daily check | Required | +18 |
| Leaf cleaning | Every 3 days | Optional | +10 |
| Meditation | Anytime | Optional | +20 |
| Koi feeding | Daily | Optional | +5 |

### Death & Recovery
| State | Trigger | Visual | Recovery |
|-------|---------|--------|----------|
| Healthy | Water fresh | Clear water, vibrant | N/A |
| Murky | 2 days no care | Water darkening | Clean water |
| Struggling | 3 days no care | Leaves wilting | Intensive care |
| Dormant | 5 days no care | Retreats to rhizome | 1 week recovery |

**Special**: Lotus is resilient! Even "dead" lotus has rhizome that can regenerate in 1-2 weeks.
