## 12. Hoa Hồng (Rose) - "The Passionate Teacher"

### Quick Stats
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|

### Habit Psychology
- **Phù hợp cho**: Thói quen đang trong giai đoạn hình thành (Day 1-66), thói quen cần sự chú ý cao
- **Dạy user**: "The hardest habits create the strongest character"
- **Personality match**: Ambitious, willing to be challenged, values growth through difficulty

### Care Requirements

|--------|-----------|-------------------|----------|
| Watering | Daily | **Required** | +20 |
| Reflection note | Day 8+ | **Required** | +15-30 |

| Pruning | Weekly | Optional | +10 |

### Visual Progression
```
Stage 1 (Day 1-7): Small seedling with tiny leaves
  → Emotion: Hopeful beginning
  → Care: Just watering

Stage 2 (Day 8-14): Stem growing, first thorns visible
  → Emotion: Reality of commitment
  → Care: Watering + simple reflection prompt
  → Prompt: "How did today's [habit] go?" (any length)

Stage 3 (Day 15-21): More thorns, bud forming
  → Emotion: Building momentum
  → Care: Watering + required reflection (3+ words)
  → Prompt: "What made today's [habit] meaningful?"

Stage 4 (Day 22-35): Bud swelling, color showing
  → Emotion: Anticipation, can see progress
  → Care: Watering + weekly summary reflection
  → Prompt: "Reflect on your week with [habit]"

Stage 5 (Day 36-45): Bud opening slowly
  → Emotion: Almost there, staying strong
  → Care: Continue routine

FIRST BLOOM (Day 45): Rose opens!
  → Emotion: Pride, accomplishment
  → MAJOR CELEBRATION

Mature (Day 45+): Full rose, periodic reblooming
  → New bloom every 21 days of consistency
  → Each bloom different color based on care quality
```

### Unique Mechanics

**1. Progressive Reflection System**
```
Day 1-7:   Watering only (build basic rhythm)
Day 8-14:  Watering + "How did it go?" prompt
           → Any response accepted
           → Teaches: reflection is part of habit

Day 15-21: Watering + Required reflection (3+ words)
           → Skip = Rose wilts FASTER (-20% moisture/day extra)
           → Quality detected: length, sentiment analysis
           → Good reflection = visual brightness bonus

Day 22+:   Watering + Weekly summary
           → Once per week, deeper reflection
           → "What have you learned about yourself?"
           → Feeds into "Rose Journal" keepsake
```

**2. Thorn Challenge System**
- Random "thorn" challenges appear (2-3 per month)
- Example challenges:
  - "Write a longer reflection today (50+ words)"
  - "Water your rose at a different time"
  - "Share your progress with someone"
- Completing challenge = thorn turns GOLD
- 5 golden thorns = special achievement
- Challenges are OPTIONAL but rewarding

**3. Bloom Color Based on Care**
| Color | How to Earn | Meaning |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Red | Standard good care | Classic beauty |
| Pink | Morning waterings (6-9 AM) | Gentle, hopeful |
| White | Perfect consistency (no misses) | Purity, dedication |
| Yellow | High streak (30+ days) | Joy, achievement |
| Orange | Recovered from wilt 2+ times | Resilience |
| Black | Recovered from near-death | Rare, prized, "Scar Rose" |
| Blue | Secret: 3 golden thorns + 45 days | Ultra rare |

**4. Graduation System**
- At Day 66 (habit formation milestone):
  - System celebrates: "Your habit is forming!"
  - Option to "graduate" Rose to maintenance mode
  - Maintenance mode: Less demanding, auto-water option
  - Can plant new Rose for same habit (challenge again)
  - Or switch to Lavender (easier maintenance)

```
Stage 1 (Day 1-10): Small leaves emerging
  → Emotion: Delicate beginning

Stage 2 (Day 11-25): Multiple leaves, aerial roots visible
  → Emotion: Building foundation

Stage 3 (Day 26-40): Flower stem (spike) emerging
  → Emotion: Excitement - bloom is coming!

  → Emotion: Anticipation builds

  → Day 55: First bud opens

  → Blooms last 2-3 weeks before fading
```

### Unique Mechanics

**1. Morning Watering Bonus**
- Watering before 9 AM = 1.5x XP
- Creates real morning routine anchor
- "Early Bird" streak tracking
- 20 morning waterings = "Morning Master" badge

**2. Temperature Sensitivity**
- Checks real weather OR simulated seasons
- Hot day (>30°C): Needs misting (extra action)
- Cold day (<15°C): Reduced watering needed
- Teaches: Adapt care to circumstances

**3. Root Health System**
- Hidden "root health" meter (0-100%)
- Weekly "root check" reveals status
- Healthy roots (>60%): Faster recovery from stress
- Weak roots (<40%): More fragile plant
- Roots improve with consistent care
- Root health affects bloom quality

**4. Sequential Bloom**
- Orchid buds don't open all at once
- One bud opens every 2 days during bloom phase
- Creates daily anticipation: "Which bud today?"
- Full display after all open = stunning visual

**5. Orchid Specialist Path**
After growing 3 orchids:
- Unlock "Orchid Sense" - Can see orchid mood precisely
- Unlock "Perfect Timing" - Optimal watering window shown
- Can attempt to grow rare variants
- Join "Orchid Society" community group

### Death & Recovery

| Critical | Moisture 10-29% | Yellow leaves | 3 waterings + root care |
