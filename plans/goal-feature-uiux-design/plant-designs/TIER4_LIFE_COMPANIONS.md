```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

               → "I am someone who [habit]"
               → Minimal care needed
               → Grove expands slowly
```

### Unique Mechanics

**1. Delayed Gratification Test**
- First 90 days: NOTHING visible above ground
- This is intentional - tests and builds patience
- Underground progress shown as subtle hints:
  - Soil slightly raised
  - Small insects around soil
  - "Root network" visualization (optional view)
- User learns: "Not all progress is visible"

**2. Explosive Growth Phase**
- Days 91-120: RAPID visible growth
- Each day shows dramatic change
- Creates addictive "check tomorrow" feeling
- Most visually rewarding phase in entire game
- User thinks: "The wait was worth it!"

**3. Underground Network**
- Bamboo rhizomes connect underground
- After mature, BUFFS adjacent plants:
  - +10% drought tolerance for neighbors
  - +5% XP for all nearby plants
- "Foundation of the garden" effect

**4. Flexibility Mechanic**
- During "storms" (challenge periods, bad mood)
- Bamboo visually bends but doesn't break
- Other plants may struggle
- Visual metaphor for resilience

**5. New Shoot Events**
- After mature, new shoots randomly appear
- Each shoot = same habit reinforcement
- Can have bamboo "grove" from single planting
- Represents habit becoming part of identity

### Death & Recovery
| State | Trigger | Visual | Recovery |
|-------|---------|--------|----------|
| Healthy | Moisture 50-100% | Green, swaying | N/A |
| Thirsty | Moisture 25-49% | Less movement | 1 watering |
| Stressed | Moisture 10-24% | Yellowing leaves | 2 waterings |
| Critical | Moisture 0% (pre-mature) | Brown tips | 3 waterings |
| **Near-Immortal** | After Day 180 | Survives almost anything | N/A |
| Dead | Moisture 0% for 10+ days (pre-mature only) | Dried | Rhizome survives, regrow |

**Special**: Mature bamboo (Day 180+) is VERY hard to kill. Even "dead" bamboo can regrow from rhizomes.

## 22. Cây Bonsai (Bonsai) - "The Living Art"

### Quick Stats
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Tier | 4 - Expert |
| Difficulty | Expert (Artistry) |
| Tolerance | 2 ngày không tưới |
| Growth Duration | 365 ngày để mature |
| XP Multiplier | 3.5x |
| Unlock | Level 14 OR 10 different plant types matured |

### Habit Psychology
- **Phù hợp cho**: Single, deep habit commitment; mastery over breadth
- **Dạy user**: "Mastery comes from limitation and patience"
- **Personality match**: Artist, minimalist, values depth over breadth

### Care Requirements
| Action | Frequency | Required/Optional | XP Bonus |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|----------|
| Watering | Daily | **Required** | +30 |
| Shaping decision | Monthly | **Required** | +50 |
| Wiring check | Bi-weekly | Optional | +15 |
| Pot upgrade | Quarterly | Optional | +40 |

### Visual Progression - UNIQUE PER USER
```
The Bonsai Journey is DIFFERENT for everyone:

Month 1 (Foundation):
  Seedling in training pot
  First shaping choice: Tree style
  Options: Formal upright, Informal, Cascade, etc.

Month 2-3 (Structure):
  Basic shape forming
  Trunk direction established
  First branch decisions

Month 4-6 (Character):
  Secondary branches
  Foliage pads developing
  Unique character emerging

Month 7-9 (Refinement):
  Fine details appearing
  Bark texture developing
  Getting close to vision

Month 10-12 (Maturity):
  Mature bonsai form
  Reflects all shaping choices
  Truly UNIQUE to this user

Year 2+:
  "Ancient" characteristics
  Shows aging (weathered bark, deadwood)
  Museum-quality appearance
```

### Unique Mechanics

**1. Monthly Shaping Choices**
Every month, user makes aesthetic decision:
```
Month 1: "What style should your bonsai be?"
  - Formal Upright (Chokkan): Disciplined, traditional
  - Informal Upright (Moyogi): Natural, flowing
  - Cascade (Kengai): Dramatic, artistic
  - Windswept (Fukinagashi): Resilient, weathered
  - Broom (Hokidachi): Balanced, harmonious

Month 2: "Which direction should the trunk lean?"
  - Left / Right / Center

Month 3: "Should we encourage more branches?"
  - Yes (fuller) / No (minimalist)

Month 4: "Add moss to the soil?"
  - Yes / No

... and so on, each month a new choice
```

**2. NO TWO BONSAIS ARE IDENTICAL**
- Choices compound over time
- Your bonsai reflects YOUR journey
- Visual representation of 12 months of decisions
- Truly personalized plant

**3. "Age Rings" and Wisdom**
- Trunk shows care history
- Weathered areas = recovered from stress
- Smooth areas = consistent care
- Deadwood (jin) = survived crisis
- Each mark tells a story

**4. Reflection Journal**
- Each shaping choice prompts reflection
- "Why did you choose left?"
- "What does minimalist mean to you?"
- Answers stored in "Bonsai Journal"
- After 1 year, read back all reflections

**5. The Art of Limitation**
- Bonsai is about what you DON'T do
- Pruning = removing what's unnecessary
- Prompts: "What would you prune from your life?"
- Teaches intentional simplification

### Death & Recovery
| State | Trigger | Visual | Recovery |
|-------|---------|--------|----------|
| Healthy | Moisture 60-100% | Vibrant | N/A |
| Thirsty | Moisture 30-59% | Slight wilt | 1 watering |
| Stressed | Moisture 10-29% | Leaf drop | 2 waterings |
| Critical | Moisture 0% | Major leaf loss | 3 waterings + shaping skip |
| Scarred | Recovered from critical | Shows "jin" (deadwood) | Permanent character mark |

**Bonsai NEVER fully dies** - even worst neglect creates "dramatic deadwood" rather than death. The struggle becomes part of the art.
