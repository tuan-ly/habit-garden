## 1. Co (Grass) - "The Eternal Beginner"

### Quick Stats
- **Tier**: 1
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Tolerance**: 5 days without water
- **Growth Duration**: 21 days to mature
- **XP Multiplier**: 1.0x

### Habit Psychology
- **Phu hop cho**: First-time habit builders, anxiety-prone users, those recovering from burnout
- **Day user**: Habits start small - even the tiniest effort counts
- **Personality match**: The Overwhelmed Starter, The Perfectionist (learning to accept "good enough")

### Care Requirements
| Action | Frequency | Required/Optional | XP Bonus |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|----------|
| Watering | Daily | Required | +10 |
| Note | Any | Optional | +5 |
| Check-in | Morning/Evening | Optional | +3 |

### Visual Progression (6 stages)
- **Stage 1 (Day 1-3)**: Bare soil with tiny green specks. Emotion: Hope, anticipation
- **Stage 2 (Day 4-7)**: Short grass blades emerging, pale green. Emotion: First success
- **Stage 3 (Day 8-11)**: Grass thickening, deeper green. Emotion: Building momentum
- **Stage 4 (Day 12-15)**: Lush grass patch, some blades taller. Emotion: Confidence
- **Stage 5 (Day 16-20)**: Dense, vibrant lawn with gentle sway animation. Emotion: Pride
- **Mature (Day 21+)**: Perfect grass patch with occasional butterfly visitor. Emotion: Accomplishment

### Unique Mechanics
1. **Immortal Mode**: Grass never truly dies - only turns brown/dormant. Water once to revive instantly.
2. **Patch Expansion**: At 100% growth, grass visually "spreads" slightly beyond its tile (cosmetic).
3. **Weather Resilient**: Rain days give 2x moisture boost (grass loves rain).

### Death & Recovery
- **Wilt trigger**: 3 days without water (turns pale)
- **Death trigger**: Never dies - enters "dormant" brown state after 7 days
- **Recovery method**: Single watering revives to previous health
- **Death consequence**: N/A - grass is forgiving by design

### Rewards & Achievements
- **Base completion**: 100 XP + "First Sprout" badge
- **Special achievements**:
  - "Lawn Care 101" - Keep grass healthy for 21 days
  - "Green Thumb Beginner" - First plant to mature
- **Unlocks**: Tier 2 plants become visible in shop

### Variants & Colors
- **Default**: Classic green lawn
- **Earned variants**:
  - "Morning Dew" (sparkle effect) - 7-day streak
  - "Wild Meadow" (mixed with tiny flowers) - 30-day streak
- **Premium variants**: "Zen Garden" moss, "Sports Field" striped

### Easter Eggs
- Water at exactly 6:00 AM for 7 days: Grass sparkles with dew drops permanently
- Name your grass "Lawn" - gets a tiny "Keep Off" sign decoration

### Lore
> "Every great garden began with a single blade of grass. It doesn't ask for much - just a little attention each day. In return, it teaches you that consistency matters more than perfection."

### Reflection Prompts
- **Day 7**: "What's one small thing you did consistently this week?"
- **Day 14**: "How does it feel to see something grow because of your care?"
- **Day 21**: "You've built your first foundation. What's next?"
